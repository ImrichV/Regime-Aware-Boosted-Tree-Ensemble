# Known Limitations

- The original Stooq universe is dominated by securities still active near the archive end date.
- Exact vendor adjustment policy is not independently verified.
- Daily bars cannot resolve intraday path or stop/target ordering without a conservative assumption.
- Borrow availability and locate costs are absent for short candidates.
- The public release excludes private playbook thresholds and generated artifacts.
- The public boosted-tree layer has not been represented as an accepted production model.
- No point-in-time fundamental, news, event, or order-book data is included.
- No accepted live portfolio simulator or execution engine is included.
