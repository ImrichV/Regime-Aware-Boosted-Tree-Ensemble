# Public Scope and Disclosure Boundary

## Published

- canonical parsing and validation infrastructure;
- causal feature and candidate contracts;
- deterministic storage and run manifests;
- outcome and research-table infrastructure;
- fixed baseline machinery;
- synthetic candidate and specialist-ensemble examples;
- high-level family catalogue and validation methodology.

## Not published

- exact private candidate thresholds;
- full private playbook implementations;
- raw market archives;
- large feature, candidate, outcome, and OOF prediction shards;
- ticker-level model scores and trade lists;
- private performance investigations;
- production execution, risk, sizing, and portfolio decisions;
- credentials, cloud details, or personal information.
