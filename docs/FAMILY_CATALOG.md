# Private Family Catalogue — Public Descriptions

The recovered archive contains eleven frozen candidate families. This public document describes their conceptual roles without exposing the exact thresholds or full production generator code.

| Family | Direction | Public description |
|---|---|---|
| Relative-strength pullback | Long | Established absolute and market-relative strength followed by a controlled retracement. |
| Consolidation breakout | Long | Break through prior resistance after a measured contraction and confirmation. |
| Volatility-contraction continuation | Long | Trend continuation emerging from progressively compressed price behaviour. |
| Oversold recovery | Long | Recovery from a short-term oversold state without requiring a full trend breakout. |
| Blow-off / failed move | Short | Material upside extension followed by evidence that the move failed. |
| Range mean reversion | Long | Reversion candidate near the lower part of a bounded range. |
| Relative-weakness breakdown | Short | Persistent market-relative weakness followed by support failure. |
| Relief-rally failure | Short | Weak-trend instrument rallies into resistance and rejects. |
| Ordinary failed breakout | Short | Conventional false breakout or bull trap without extreme blow-off conditions. |
| Orderly trend continuation | Long | Controlled retracement or pause inside an established orderly trend. |
| Capitulation undercut / reclaim | Long | Sharp downside stress followed by a reclaim of a meaningful reference level. |

Candidate-family acceptance means that definitions were causal, reproducible, and operationally validated. It does not mean that any family was proven profitable.
